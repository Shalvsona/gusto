Our Story

Fast Food the Way It Should Be

Gusto started as a small corner joint with one grill, two cooks, and an obsession with flavour. We believed people deserved fast food that didn't feel like a compromise.

Today, we serve hundreds of hungry guests every day — but our promise stays the same. Every patty is fresh, every sauce is house-made, and every order gets our full attention.
